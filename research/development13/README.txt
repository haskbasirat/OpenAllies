AXIS & ALLIES RUST - DEVELOPMENT 13 (BEVY) - TEST BUILD
DIAGNOSTIC / UNFINISHED

WHAT THIS IS
The first playable custom battle of the Rust recreation, drawn with Bevy.
It follows the original game's code and data closely (not byte for byte),
using the original menus, HUD, maps, models, animations and rules from your
installed game.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Run Start.cmd.
2. Main menu -> Single Player -> Custom.
3. On the original setup screen choose Random Map (map type, size, sides,
   terrain sliders) or a scenario map such as Fall of Berlin, set the
   players, nations, teams and computer generals, then Launch.
4. On the loading screen click Continue when it finishes loading.
5. Play:
   - Left click selects a building, a regiment (click a soldier or its
     banner), drag a box to select several, double click selects the same
     kind.
   - Right click moves; right click an enemy attacks.
   - Select a building to recruit with the buttons on the right of the HUD.
   - Recruited HQ and depot trucks deploy into buildings with Deploy.
   - Camera: arrow keys or the screen edges to scroll, mouse wheel or
     Page Up/Page Down to zoom, the original hotkeys.

WHAT WORKS
- The original menus, setup screen, random map generator, loading screen
  and in-game HUD (economy bar, unit panel, minimap, action buttons).
- Economy, recruiting, research, truck deployment, supply, city capture,
  victory, computer opponents (the original AI generals and difficulty).
- Movement in formations, combat with the original damage, morale,
  routing, entrenchment, experience, death animations.
- Floating unit banners, selection health bars, waving flags, building
  ground patches, some weapon effects.

NOT FINISHED (expect gaps)
- Sound and many effects are incomplete.
- Campaign, World War II, Multiplayer, Editor, Film and saved games.
- Tooltips, minimap fog, some HUD buttons (rally point, sell...).
- Exact behaviour still has to be compared with the original side by side.

REQUIREMENTS
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, Vulkan or DirectX 12 graphics driver.
- Your installed original Axis & Allies at E:\Games\Axis and Alies with the
  reconstruction folder beside rust-rebuild, as for earlier builds.
- Keep AxisAlliesBevy.exe and the assets-cache folder together.

Earlier Development builds are preserved unchanged in release\.
