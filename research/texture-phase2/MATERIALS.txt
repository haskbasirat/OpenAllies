Original material metadata correction scope
==========================================

The exporter reads the existing 1,847-model converted manifest and the corresponding
original Data NIF files. It does not edit those files or their textures. Every
existing converted mesh is matched in order to its original hierarchy, name,
vertex count and triangle count. Both whole-file SHA256 values are recorded.
The consumer must reject missing/mismatched records instead of guessing.

The corrections address two original engine tags:
  * Sg and Sg2 designate native shadow proxy geometry. Such meshes are not normal
    colored scene surfaces and are omitted from this ordinary geometry pass.
  * RepColor selects the original texture-alpha/team-color material behavior.
    Its presence is significant even if a named extra has a value of zero.

ProcessObject 0x879A90 copies a parent context, clears the relevant inherited
flags at each non-NiGeometry object (0x879B72..0x879B86), processes the current
object's own tags, and passes this context to children (0x87A7A1..0x87A7BC).
A tag on a NiNode therefore reaches an immediate geometry child. It is not
unconditionally inherited across a tagless intermediate NiNode.

Before checking tag presence, 0x8790B0 normalizes known names from CRLF-delimited
NiStringExtraData lines; 0x877C50 uses exact name length and a case-insensitive
comparison. It does not generally trim whitespace or split bare LF. Its scan
retains the last '=' and does not flush a final unterminated '='. 0x871CC0 then
checks exact named-extra presence, followed by case-insensitive whole-string
values. The ten-name normalization table includes RepColor, Sg and Sg2.
The corpus's effective string tags are bare repcolor/Sg/Sg2/SG values. A string
extra consumed entirely by normalization loses its former name before the
canonical new names are attached; for example name RepColor/value SG becomes
a shadow tag. Ambiguous combinations of multiple conflicting named strings,
multiple normalized strings with non-bare material tags, or a trailing '='
after normalized lines require a separate mutable-array
audit and are rejected. This is not a general replacement for Gamebryo loading.
These byte ranges and the material stage operations are separately recorded in
research/phase2-textures/repcolor-native-state-oracle.json and its assembly files.

Targeted original evidence:
  GBRCorps: one regular textured RepColor mesh.
  GBRCorpsPack: 72 existing converted meshes; 15 shadow-tagged proxy meshes were
    formerly rendered as red/green strips, and ten roof meshes require RepColor
    supplied by their immediate parent UserPropBuffer tags.
  GBRDepotOil: all three existing converted meshes remain. The mesh named Sg01
    has RepColor and no shadow tag. A name-only Sg prefix rule would be wrong.

No general red/untextured-mesh filter, name-prefix shadow filter, new UV edit,
or texture-image mutation is applied. The old converter's eligibility/nearest
LOD traversal is reproduced only to identify its existing rows. This audit
does not restore geometry that the historical converter already omitted, and
does not claim complete native animation, visibility, LOD or shadow rendering.
The renderer separately applies the audited native RepColor expression and
opaque output-alpha correction. Screenshot acceptance belongs to the parent.
