# Code of Conduct

OpenAllies is a small fan project built by people who care about an old game. We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project lives) to be treated with respect.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in OpenAllies a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that keep this community open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before;
- respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- owning our mistakes, apologising to those they affect, and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and the community, not just for ourselves.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission;
- any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities

The maintainers clarify and enforce these standards. They will take fair and appropriate action in response to any behaviour they find inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and will say why when it is appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public: for example, by posting from an official account or acting as a representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the project maintainer, [@haskbasirat](https://github.com/haskbasirat). If you can't find a private way to reach them, open an issue that asks for a private contact, without any details of the incident.

All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of whoever reports an incident.

## Enforcement

The maintainers follow these steps when deciding what to do about a breach of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other behaviour that is unprofessional or unwelcome: a private, written warning that explains what happened and why it was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or a series of smaller ones: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour. For a set period, there is no interaction with the people involved, including unrequested contact with the maintainers. Breaking these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious breach, including sustained inappropriate behaviour: a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community. Breaking these terms may lead to a permanent ban.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of breaches, harassment of an individual, or aggression towards or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from any public interaction with the community.
