  actor_world has owned ground construction and explicit remaining callbacks.

  contain the native world/admin clock and ordered scheduling machinery.

  production, zones and RTS AI have substantial component ports with separate
  native oracle fixtures. Their World/Effects traits still require integration.

  These are reusable by a real local session before internet work.
- Development08 displays original menus and assets. aa-client does not depend
  on aa-runtime or aa-netcode, and RequestCustom/actual match entry remains
  disabled. Live preview uses its own diagnostic actor/time, not this world.
